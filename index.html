Pulsed laser annealing of silicon: uncertainty, optimization and phase-field modelling

M.Tech thesis. Built an enthalpy-based solver for nanosecond laser annealing (Beer-Lambert absorption, finite volume method, Newton-Raphson), then asked which input uncertainty matters most.

99.99%of melt-depth uncertainty traced to real permittivity (reflectivity)
3,000Latin Hypercube simulations across three permittivity datasets
>95%target success rate inside a 40% tolerance window

Stage 2 couples the solver to a 3-D anisotropic phase-field grain-growth model in FEniCS, with interface mobility calibrated by kinetic Monte Carlo (MulSKIPS) and a surrogate model for crystal orientation.
